# ShiftMed User Analysis

## Overview
Analyze user data from the app ShiftMed. In our analysis, we located meaningful customer groups and discussed strategic recommendations with our stakeholders based on our findings.

## Objective
Locate meaningful customer groups and identify behaviors of users in them. Classify what behaviors predict long term retention on the ShiftMed app.

## Data
ShiftMed supplied us with data of their users, facilities the utilized, frequency of certain nurses at certain facilities, etc., so we had multiple spreadsheets, some with 50,000+ rows. We utilized a combination of all of our spreadsheets in our analysis.

## Tools Used
R. Utilzed ggplot2 and tidyverse packages.

## Methods
- Data cleaning
- Exploratory analysis
- Data Visualization
- Communication and Presentation
